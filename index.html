HOW TO PLAY

1. MoveTouch and hold the LEFT or RIGHT half of the screen. Let go to stop.
2. BounceSpongeBob jumps automatically. Land on platforms to keep climbing.
3. CollectCoins, bubbles, jellyfish tokens, golden spatulas and treasure chests.
4. AvoidJellyfish, urchins, sea mines, dangerous bubbles and falling rocks cost half of the coins you collected this run. With no coins left, the run ends.

Power-ups

  • Super Bounce stronger bounces
  • Bubble Shield blocks one hit
  • Jellyfish Magnet pulls in coins
  • Speed Boost faster sideways movement
  • Double Coins coins count twice
  • Slow Motion enemies slow down
  • Coin Guard your next hit costs no coins
  • Mega Bounce next bounce launches very high

Zones

Bikini Bottom → Jellyfish Fields → Goo Lagoon → Krusty Krab Area → Rock Bottom. Each zone is harder and more challenging.
